Waterproofing my house

This project is about waterproofing my house by building a French drain inside the crawlspace. As you can imagine it involves a lot of crawling and digging.

1. Taking out all the insulation

The house has never been waterproofed, so the insulation was falling down./p>

2. Digging trenches

The hardest part of making a french drain is about the digging. I used a pic axe to soften the soil and then shovel and take the soil out.

3. Lay the non woven cloth

After digging the trench around the inside perimeter of the crawlspace, I laid a non woven cloth that will help filter the water by blocking the sand from entering the drain pipe.

4. Add gravel, and Put the drain pipe.

At this point I added gravel, installed the perforated 4 inch drain pipe inside the trench on top of the non woven cloth and gravel.

5. Dig the sump pump pit

This step involve digging the sump pump pit, install the basin, the sump pump and set it up with the tubbing.

6. Fold the cloth and add more gravel on top

I folded the cloth and added more gravel on top. By the way, the purpose of the gravel is to further filter the water by blocking the sand and anything other than water.

7. At this point I need more gravel. I will update later!
